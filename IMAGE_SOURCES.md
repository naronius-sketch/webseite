# Demo image sources

These locally stored images illustrate the Makao website prototype. They do not document the location, condition, ownership or availability of any property in Kenya. Property names, locations and listing data used with them are fictional examples. The hero deliberately reuses `public/images/property-5.jpg`, the tropical villa image.

The images were downloaded from public website demonstration repositories. Public availability alone does not establish unrestricted rights to the underlying photographs. Before commercial publication, confirm the original photograph rights or replace the images with owned or properly licensed photographs, including any required attribution.

## Repository statements

The [Realvine README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codewithsadee/realvine/master/README.md) states: “This project is **free to use** and does not contains any license.” This statement concerns the demonstration project; it does not identify the photographer or independently establish licensing of `property-5.jpg`. No original photo source or specific photo license was established during setup.
